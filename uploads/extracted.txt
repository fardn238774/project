Project Overview
AutoBD is a comprehensive web platform unifying the four ways Bangladeshi buyers acquire
cars — brand-new dealership purchases, peer-to-peer used car transactions, Japanese
reconditioned vehicle imports via auction, and aftermarket modification sourcing — into a single
trusted marketplace with a consistent, transparent cost calculation engine running across all four
pillars.
The platform's core differentiator is its Reconditioned Auction section, which operates as a
marketplace of competing licensed bidding organizations rather than a single in-house import
team. Buyers select their preferred agent based on track record, ratings, fee structure, and
turnaround time, then collaborate with that agent through a live bidding dashboard to win cars at
Japanese auctions. This model — closer to Upwork than to SAT Japan or BE FORWARD —
creates competitive pressure among agents on service quality and gives buyers genuine choice
and accountability. Two Bangladesh-specific features further distinguish the platform: Container
Pooling (automatically grouping buyers into shared shipping containers to cut per-car freight
costs by up to 30%) and a BRTA Registration Paper Value Tracker (surfacing the currently
opaque resale value of a car's remaining import-eligible registration life).
Tech Stack:
Language: TypeScript, JavaScript
Framework: Next.js
Styling: TailwindCSS
Database: PostgreSQL
ORM: Prisma
Deployment: Vercel
External APIs:
OpenAI API: For the AI-powered car recommendation engine and Dream Car Research Hub
intelligence (Member A).
SSLCommerz / bKash API: For escrow payment processing and agent fee collection (Member
D).
Google Maps API: For dealership/service center locator and live vessel position tracking
(Members A & C).
Exchange Rate / Currency API (e.g., Open Exchange Rates): For live JPY-to-BDT conversion in
the landed cost calculator and auction dashboard (Member D).
User Roles:
1. Buyer: The primary user — browses all four pillars, selects a bidding organization,
participates in auctions, purchases used cars, orders modifications, and tracks shipments.
2. Bidding Organization: A licensed Japanese auction agent — applies for platform approval,
manages their public profile, conducts live bidding on behalf of assigned buyers, and responds to
ratings and disputes.
3. Admin: System super-user — approves bidding organization applications, manages duty rate
tables and car database, monitors platform analytics, and adjudicates disputes.

Functional Requirements
SL 	Common Workflows
1 	Registration and Login System: Users can create an account by selecting their
role: Buyer (individual purchasing a new, used, or reconditioned car), Bidding
Organization (a licensed Japanese auction agent applying for platform
partnership), or Administrator. Buyers register with name, email, phone, and
password. Bidding Organizations complete a multi-step application form covering
company name, license number, track record, and fee structure — their account
remains inactive until approved by an admin. All users log in securely to access
their role-specific dashboards.
2 	Admin Role & Access Management: The administrator reviews and approves or
rejects Bidding Organization applications, verifies submitted license details and
track records, and can suspend any active organization account if complaints or
rating thresholds are breached. The admin also manages the platform's car
brand/model database, moderates used-car listings flagged by buyers, monitors
platform-wide analytics (total bids placed, successful imports, active listings,
revenue by pillar), and configures NBR duty rate tables used by the unified landed
cost calculator.

Member
D
Unified Landed Cost
Calculator
A consistent cost calculation engine available across all
four platform pillars — new cars, used cars,
reconditioned imports, and modification bundles. For
reconditioned cars: bid price (in JPY) is converted to
BDT at live exchange rates, then NBR import duty is
applied based on engine CC and vehicle age (using the
admin-maintained duty rate table), plus shipping
estimate, agent fee, and port handling — giving a single
total landed cost figure. The calculator updates in real
time as the bid price changes during a live auction. For
new and used cars, it shows total on-road price
including registration and token tax. This is the single
most-used tool across the platform and is intentionally
kept consistent so users always trust the number they
see.

Reconditioned Car
Auction Interface
The core auction experience for buyers who have
selected a bidding organization. Buyers specify their
target car using a structured form: make, model, year
range, budget ceiling, maximum mileage, and must-
have features, with an option to attach reference photos
or links. The live bidding dashboard shows the current

JPY and live BDT equivalent, time remaining, and the
number of active bidders. A real-time chat and call-log

bidder for live decisions (e.g., "go up to ¥750,000").
Buyers receive in-app and SMS alerts when a matching
lot appears or when their bidder is about to place a bid.
Member
C
Container Pooling —
Group Shipping
Coordination
A Bangladesh-specific feature addressing the high per-
car shipping cost faced by individual buyers. After a
buyer wins a lot at auction, the system checks for other
buyers whose cars are at the same origin port (e.g.,
Yokohama) within a compatible shipping window and
size class. Eligible buyers are shown a prompt: "3 open
slots on a Yokohama→Chattogram container departing
in 11 days — join to reduce your shipping cost by
approximately 30%." Buyers who opt in are grouped
into a shared container managed by the platform's
logistics partner. The feature improves as the platform
grows (more simultaneous imports = better pool
matches), creating a natural network-effect moat that
single-vendor importers cannot replicate.

A natural-language assistant that helps buyers who are
unsure of what to look for. A buyer can describe their
needs in plain text (e.g., "I want a fuel-efficient family
car under 25 lakh taka, preferably Japanese, good for
Dhaka traffic") and the system uses the OpenAI API to
extract requirements, map them to available inventory
across all three pillars (new, used, reconditioned), and
return a ranked shortlist with reasoning for each
recommendation. The assistant also flags trade-offs

cost in BD") using data from the Dream Car Research
Hub. Buyers can refine suggestions through follow-up
messages.

A catalog browsing section for BRTA-legal cosmetic

Member
C
Shipment & Import
Status Tracker
(Google Maps API)
A real-time shipment tracking dashboard for buyers
with an active import order. Stages tracked include:
auction win confirmed → payment received → vehicle
collected at Japanese port → vessel departed → vessel
in transit → arrived at Chattogram port → customs
clearance → ready for delivery. Each stage is
timestamped and displayed on a visual progress
timeline. A map view (Google Maps API) shows the
vessel's last known position during transit. SMS and in-
app notifications are sent automatically at each stage
transition. Buyers with container-pooled shipments see
all cars in their shared container on the same tracker.
Member
D
BRTA Registration
Paper Value Tracker
A Bangladesh-specific feature addressing a real,
currently opaque market factor: a car's BRTA
registration paper carries separate resale value tied to
NBR import age-quota rules that shift with each
national budget. For any used or reconditioned listing,
this tool displays how many years of import-eligible
registration life remain before the car ages out of
common resale categories, along with a historical view
of how NBR's import age-limit policy has changed
across past budgets. This converts a word-of-mouth,
informal factor into a transparent, data-backed display
on every relevant listing — a genuine information
advantage for buyers that no existing platform currently
offers.
Member
A
Accident History &
Inspection
Verification Report
For used car listings, sellers can upload auction sheets,
previous inspection reports, and ownership transfer
documents. The platform's verification layer cross-
references the chassis number (VIN) against a partner
database of accident records and flags any history
found. Verified listings display an "Accident History:
None Found" or "1 Incident Recorded" badge. Buyers
can optionally request a third-party physical inspection
through a partner garage network, booked and paid
directly through the platform. The inspection report is
uploaded to the listing and permanently visible to all
subsequent buyers if the car is relisted — creating a
persistent trust record tied to the chassis number.
